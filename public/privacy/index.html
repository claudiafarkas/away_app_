Privacy Policy

Away values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Away app.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in, we collect your name and email address to authenticate your account. You may also add a profile photo.
  • User Content: Instagram or other video links you paste or share into Away, captions we read from those posts, saved locations, map pins, folders, and related metadata.
  • Place Data: We send place names and addresses to Google Maps geocoding so we can save latitude, longitude, city, and country for pins you keep.
  • Location Information: Location data associated with places you save or view. Away does not track real-time background location.
  • Usage & Diagnostics: Basic usage, crash, and performance data to improve stability.

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate users
  • Parse links you provide so we can extract places mentioned in a post
  • Save and display your pins, photos, and related content
  • Maintain performance and security

Data Sharing

Away does not sell personal data. We use trusted third-party services only for core functionality: Firebase (authentication, database, and file storage), Google Maps (maps and geocoding), and a parsing provider that fetches public Instagram post data you ask us to import.

Tracking

Away does not track users across apps or websites and does not use data for targeted advertising.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

Your Choices

You may manage your profile, sign out, or permanently delete your account and saved places from Login & security in the app.
